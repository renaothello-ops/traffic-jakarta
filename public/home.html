Languages

The app supports 20 interface languages (including English, Bahasa Indonesia, Japanese, Korean, Chinese, Thai, Arabic, and European languages). Language quality varies by locale — see in-app settings.

Coverage

Built for drivers and commuters in Jakarta and surrounding areas. Map and traffic data depend on your device location permission and third-party providers (TomTom, Google Places when configured on the server).
