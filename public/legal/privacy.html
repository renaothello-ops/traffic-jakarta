Overview

Traffic Jakarta (“the Service”) provides a traffic map, community reports, messaging, and related features. This draft describes categories of data the app may process.

Data we may collect

  • Account: Google sign-in identifiers, display name, username, profile settings.
  • Location: When you grant permission, GPS or browser geolocation for map, routing, and nearby reports.
  • Community content: Reports, comments, votes, photos you submit (may be public or follower-only per your settings).
  • Messages: Private conversations, requests, and attachments between users subject to privacy rules.
  • Technical: Device/browser type, app logs, Firebase authentication tokens.

How data is used

  • Display traffic maps, routes, and community information
  • Authenticate users and enforce privacy (private accounts, blocks, message permissions)
  • Send in-app notifications you enable
  • Optional anonymized ML snapshots if you opt in under Privacy settings

Storage & processors

Data is stored using Google Firebase (Firestore, Authentication, Storage when enabled). Map/traffic/routing may call TomTom and Google Places via server-side API keys. [Owner: list subprocessors and regions.]

Your choices

  • Location can be disabled in Privacy settings (some features will not work).
  • Account deletion is available in Profile settings (see in-app flow).
  • Block and report users via in-app tools.
